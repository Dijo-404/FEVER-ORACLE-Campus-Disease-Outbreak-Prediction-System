## 1. **FEVER ORACLE: Campus Disease Outbreak Prediction System**

### Problem Statement
Illness clusters on campus go undetected until widespread transmission occurs. By the time students seek help, dozens may be infected. Early warning systems could enable rapid intervention.

### Community Challenge
- Students often hide illness symptoms to avoid missing classes
- Campus health centers lack predictive capabilities
- No real-time tracking of symptom patterns across campus
- Manual reporting delays outbreak detection by days

### Proposed Solution
**FEVER ORACLE** – An AI-driven predictive system that:
- Collects anonymous symptom data via optional mobile app or QR code surveys
- Uses Vertex AI to analyze patterns and detect emerging outbreak clusters
- Predicts outbreak probability 5-7 days in advance
- Provides campus health authorities with geospatial heat maps
- Triggers automated alerts and resource allocation

### Google Technologies
- **Vertex AI** – Predictive model training on disease data patterns
- **BigQuery** – Real-time data aggregation from multiple campus locations
- **Google Cloud Run** – Serverless backend for instant alert triggering
- **Gemini API** – Natural language processing for health report analysis
- **Cloud Pub/Sub** – Event-driven architecture for real-time alerts
- **Google Maps** – Geospatial visualization of outbreak clusters

### Implementation
- Phase 1: Survey tool using Google Forms + Cloud Functions
- Phase 2: Mobile app in Flutter collecting optional symptom data
- Phase 3: ML model trained on historical campus health data
- Phase 4: Dashboard for campus health officials

### Expected Impact
- 70-80% reduction in outbreak severity through early intervention
- Measurable economic savings from prevented illness clusters
- Resource optimization for campus health services
- Publishable research on campus epidemiology

### Measurable KPIs
- Days of advance warning achieved
- Percentage of cases prevented through early notification
- Campus community adoption rate
